## The slip-margin arithmetic

Required tangential force `|F_t| = m |a + g|`, with the mass from `fruits.py:977` (`rho * 4/3 pi r^3 * 0.82`) at each class's mid diameter:

| class | d (mm) | m (g) | m g (N) | +1.0 m/s^2 (N) | +5.83 m/s^2 (N) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| strawberry | 37.5 | 15.8 | 0.16 | 0.17 | 0.25 |
| lychee | 33.0 | 13.1 | 0.13 | 0.14 | 0.21 |
| kiwi | 59.0 | 79.4 | 0.78 | 0.86 | 1.24 |
| tomato | 59.0 | 68.8 | 0.67 | 0.74 | 1.08 |
| apple | 66.0 | 104.9 | 1.03 | 1.13 | 1.64 |
| orange | 65.0 | 106.1 | 1.04 | 1.15 | 1.66 |
| peach | 64.0 | 92.3 | 0.91 | 1.00 | 1.44 |
| pear | 64.0 | 99.0 | 0.97 | 1.07 | 1.55 |

The heaviest class is **orange** at 106 g (65 mm), so the worst-case demand is **1.04 N** static and **1.66 N** at the largest single-tick acceleration the carry legs report (`|a|max` 5.83 m/s^2, `logs/p39/p3/accept_C.log`; the *commanded* profile caps are `FRUIT_DYNAMIC_LIFT_AMAX`=1.0 and `FRUIT_DYNAMIC_PLACE_AMAX`=1.089, `tasks.py:2913/2939`)

Capacity at the measured `N = 4.4 N` (`mu_eff * N`, `mu_eff` = the friction the pad-fruit pair actually combines to):

| `mu_eff` | where it comes from | capacity (N) | margin vs 1.04 N | margin vs 1.66 N |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.45 | `min` combine x the lowest fruit mu (`meshes.py` pear/apple 0.45) | 1.98 | 1.9x | 1.2x |
| 1.05 | `min` combine x the highest fruit mu (kiwi 1.05) | 4.62 | 4.4x | 2.8x |
| 1.20 | `FRUIT_MU_SAFETY` 0.6 x `FRUIT_FINGER_MU` 2.0 (the carry profile's own model) | 5.28 | 5.1x | 3.2x |
| 1.49 | `average` combine, (2.0 + 0.97)/2 | 6.56 | 6.3x | 4.0x |
| 2.00 | `max` combine | 8.80 | 8.5x | 5.3x |

**Normal force for a target margin** (at the 1.66 N transient demand):

| `mu_eff` | N for 2x (N) | N for 5x (N) | inside the 10 N/joint cap? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.45 | 7.4 | 18.4 | 2x only |
| 1.05 | 3.2 | 7.9 | both |
| 1.20 | 2.8 | 6.9 | both |
| 1.49 | 2.2 | 5.6 | both |
| 2.00 | 1.7 | 4.1 | both |

The measured `N` is **4.4 N**, i.e. 44% of one joint's 10 N cap (`drive:linear:physics:maxForce = 10`, `assets/openarm_flat/openarm_flat_deinst.usda:3802`) - **the shipped pinch does not reach the actuator's limit**: its force is set by where the close's freeze stops, not by the drive cap (see GRIP_CENSUS.md section 2).
