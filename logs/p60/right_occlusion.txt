## H. The lever: is there a right-arm park to test?
- `configs/arm_parks.json` (md5 42ab4fb7) carries `parks.['back', 'ready', 'side_deep']`, and every entry's `right` is null: back->None, ready->None, side_deep->None
- `rl_env.py` already resolves a right vector with **no code change**: the loader loops `for name in ("left", "right")` and stores `entry[name]` when non-null (`rl_env.py:1371-1374`), the reset applies `self._arm_park_poses.get(name)` (`:2569`), the P35 census reads `self._arm_park_poses.get(inactive)` (`:3414`), and `_park_poses_label` already prints the per-arm resolution (`left:back,right:ready`). So a right vector is a **config-only** addition.
- but there is nothing to remove: `right:ready` is 0.000 over the whole delivered band, and it is already at the floor of the achievable set - the only right poses that cover **more** of the decision band are folds (`fold_70` 0.118, `side` 0.106, `fold_55` 0.070); `side`/`side_deep`/`fold_*` also hold (<= 0.008 rad) while `back` does not (hold 0.1741 rad, fingertip in the belt footprint ['ee_tcp']).
- verdict: **no right-arm park lever for the left's station.** A `right` vector would move a silhouette that is not in the fruit's sightline; the honest close is that this mechanism is empty, and the adjacent measurement in B2/C is what the geometry leaves standing.
