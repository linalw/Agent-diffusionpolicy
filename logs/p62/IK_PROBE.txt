The two families (candidates.py proves these, they are not fits):
  F1 yaw about world z: proj +0.5132 -> 0.0000 (dtheta 30.995 deg) ->
     -0.5132 (61.990 deg); EVERY column's z-component is invariant, so the
     attitude's relation to the vertical (finger height, pad droop) cannot
     change - the family is 'top-down-safe' by construction. It is still
     infeasible: the left's station reach is azimuth-limited (below).
  F2 roll about the frame's z column: proj delta 0.0e+00 exactly (a rotation
     about an axis fixes that axis) - it CANNOT be the lever for this
     mechanism; and it is not neutral either: the jaw axis tips out of the
     horizontal plane 0.5 -> 44.3 -> 85.3 deg at phi = 45/90 deg.
     (one pad above the other = the shipped config's own v1 failure note)
  F3 rotation about world y (the belt axis): proj delta 0.0e+00 exactly - the
     projection IS the y component, and a rotation preserves its own axis.
     A rotation about world x is the only other single-axis option and needs
     +80.94 deg (the first root of t_y cos - t_z sin = 0): proj 0.000 but the
     finger axis ends 31.5 deg BELOW horizontal and still 0.854 across the
     belt - not a top-down attitude. T0 is not any single-axis rotation of
     the shipped frame (it is 100.7 deg away and swaps which column is
     vertical); it reaches proj 0 by pointing the fingers straight down.

Verdict:
  * The yaw family is INFEASIBLE at the left station, monotonically (worst
    over the three x): the residual grows 8.3 -> 10.6 (15 deg) -> 13.6 (21.7)
    -> 19.9 (31.0) -> 48.9 (46.5) -> 118.0 mm (62.0), the wrist attitude
    error 2.0 -> 13.9 -> 55.4 deg, the binding j2 margin collapses
    0.204 -> 0.056 -> 0.000 rad, and the pads drop 30.2 -> 3.0 -> -14.2 mm
    relative to the belt top. So a yaw that removes the +0.513 belt
    projection also removes the station.
  * T0 (the reach harness's own top-down: fingers straight down, jaws exactly
    across the belt - 90.0 deg vs the shipped 31.0 deg) is the only candidate
    that reaches proj 0.000 AND solves: 6.0 / 5.8 mm at p50/station (the
    control's 5.9 / 5.7), attitude error 0.77 deg, pads +59.6..+64.6 mm
    (+14.7..+25.7 mm HIGHER than the control, so the descent's documented
    40-90 mm shortfall is not worsened by the pad geometry).
  * T0's costs are measured and must travel with the cell: the binding margin
    is +0.007 rad at j3 (the arm is at its configuration limit; unchanged at
    FRUIT_P62_RESTARTS=4, so it is the attitude, not the seed), and at the +x
    cliff x = 0.398 it reads 32.8 mm / 17.0 deg vs the control's 8.3 / 2.0 -
    i.e. T0 makes the never-held tail worse, not better.
