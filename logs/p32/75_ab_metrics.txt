5. How the interval numbers are read (M4)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  * At this N (one arm from one run, 5-6 placements = 4-5 intervals) the summary is **min/median/max**; p90 is not estimable.
  * `186`'s documented p90 rule is `sorted[int(0.9*n)]` (nearest order statistic); at n=5 that is the maximum. The first `75` used `sorted[int(0.9*n)-1]` and printed the second-largest, which is how the arm's 55.7 s outlier disappeared.
  * The placement-window rate is `60 / pooled_interval_mean` - the two headline numbers are one statistic (the Oracle's p3 note; re-derived in `scripts/200_composition_audit.py`). Do not quote a rate and an interval as independent evidence.
  * arm interval: n=5 min 18.60 median 35.50 max 55.70 mean 33.06 s; the rule-consistent p90 is 55.70 s (= max at n=5).
