### 3.3 the skip cost (the driver's own loop)
   `20_pick_place.py` advances 60 ticks (0.5 s = 60 mm of belt travel) *before* every selection, then selects;
   a `no eligible fruit` result `continue`s to the next attempt index. So a refused slot costs
   **one of the ATTEMPTS and 0.5 s of sim**, not a 15 s cycle, and it repeats until an *inside* fruit
   rises above the floor (the refused fruit never becomes legal - its x is a constant).
   selection -> `wait step=0` offset (the pre-pose's belt travel): n=39  min=0.1847 p50=0.1966 p90=0.2075 max=0.2110 m = 1.64 s
   selection-time along of the chosen fruit: n=39  min=0.4150 p50=0.5270 p90=0.6740 max=0.6780 m against the floor 0.399
   -> a refused fruit is refused for 0.3-4.7 slots (p50 2.1) **if nothing else is legal**; the slot resumes as soon as
      an inside fruit crosses the floor (supply gaps 0.10-0.35 m = 0.8-2.9 s = 2-6 slots).
   The refused fruit itself is not a lost placement: the P48 population places 0 of 6 rows above 0.39,
   so the ceiling re-allocates an attempt that was going to fail, and the cost is the wait it inserts.

# 5. THE x=0.42 CLIFF AND THE TWO-LINE SCOPE
######################################################################################################################
   two-line left : n=30  min=0.2041 p50=0.3151 p90=0.3908 max=0.3908  (>0.39 4/30)
   two-line right: n=30  min=0.2306 p50=0.2730 p90=0.3503 max=0.3674  (>0.39 0/30)
   two-line pooled: n=60  min=0.2041 p50=0.3080 p90=0.3668 max=0.3908
   The shipped selector's return order (`tasks.py select_target`, `:5797` onward):
     1. `_reach_anywhere_active()` -> `_reach_anywhere_select` (opt-in, off)
     2. floor  = pick_y + `_dynamic_select_floor()`; usable = along >= floor
     3. `lane is not None`      -> `usable[0]`          (shared-station worker)
     4. `prefer_lane is not None` -> lane/grade rule     (**the two-line**)
     5. `FRUIT_SELECT_NEAREST_ABOVE_FLOOR` (off) / `_balance_arm` -> the farthest-upstream pick
   A ceiling in the single-arm branch (step 5) is unreachable from steps 3/4: the two-line returns before it.
   And it must not reach it: the two-line's per-arm station separation is its clearance mechanism (W2),
   its eligibility is the per-arm grade (W3 pure routing), and a global x-ceiling would refuse a whole arm's
   stream off its own delivered-x tail - the R5/V1 lane-starvation repeat - while its reference
   `configs/motion_reference_twoline.json` would move. Scope: **scripted single-arm dynamic line only**.
   The 0.42 cliff: the corpus's largest chosen x is 0.3969 and its largest *usable* candidate 0.369,
   so a 0.42 ceiling refuses nothing that exists here (and 0.40 also refuses nothing): the only
   ceiling with any action on this corpus is 0.39, and its action is confined to the 0.390-0.397 window.
