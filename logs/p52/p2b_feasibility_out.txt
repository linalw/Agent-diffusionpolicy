[B5] the latency arithmetic, and the census's '13 actions' claim
    a decision costs 71.0 ms = 8.52 ACTIONS of sim
    time (one action = one control tick = 8.33 ms; 4 actions per
    decision = 33.3 ms; a 16-action chunk = 133 ms, not the census's 533 ms)
    the shipped horizon executes 4 of 16 actions -> buffer 12 actions = 100 ms = 1.4x the decision cost
    the bound: executing h of a 16-action chunk leaves (16-h) actions of
    buffer, so h <= 16 - 8.52 = 7.48 -> 7.
    The census's '13' is the same formula with 33.3 ms per action (the
    decision period) instead of 8.33 ms - 16 - 71/33.3 = 13.87 -> 13.
    And it is a CEILING on h with a chunk in flight, i.e. an async-inference
    (SAIL delta_lb) constraint; the FRONTIER E3 sentence reads it as a FLOOR
    ('s cannot go below ~13') - the direction is inverted twice over.
    Also: the shipped loop is SYNCHRONOUS (RTC/VLASH default off,
    `runtime.py:100-124`), so no chunk is ever in flight while actions
    execute: the bound does not apply at all, and h=16 is latency-free here
    (the only cost is staleness against the belt: 0.12 m/s x 0.133 s = 16 mm).
    At the phase-2 re-timing scale s the bound becomes h <= 16 - 8.52s: s=1.25 -> 5, s=1.5 -> 3 (so a horizon lengthening
    and a time-scale interact only through the async form).

[B5c] THE REPLAY RATE: the chunk is played 4x faster than it was trained

==============================================================================
  (a) the training stream's cadence is 30 Hz: the recorded stores hold one
      frame per 4 control ticks (`_record_tick` fires when `_observed_step ==
      `_step`, `rl_env.py:3771`, and `_observe` runs at `_step % 4 == 0`, 2945),
      and the chunk is built from 16 CONSECUTIVE frames
      (`policy/data.py:537`: `action[t : t+16]`) = 16 x 33.3 ms = 533 ms.
  (b) the execution cadence is 120 Hz: `act` applies one action and then
      `advance(1)` (one control tick = 8.33 ms, `rl_env.py:2979-2986`,
      1716-1724), so the same 16 actions are replayed in 16 x 8.33 = 133 ms.
      There is no hold: the drive target is overwritten every tick.
  measured (max-joint speed, rad/s):
      the demo stream (30 Hz frames, the true demo rate)   p50  0.42  p90  1.74
      the emitted chunk (120 Hz per-step, the exec rate)   p50  6.21  p90 16.56
      the ACHIEVED joint motion (120 Hz, from the store)   p50  0.64  p90  1.85
  so the command/achievement ratio is 10x, and the
  achieved rate (0.64 rad/s) is the demo's own (0.42 rad/s): the
  drive is a 0.2 s low-pass and turns the 4x replay into a dither.
  The discriminating one-line battery for phase 3 (not run here): hold each
  action for 4 ticks (execute_steps=16, or a hold counter) and measure the
  achieved rate, the residual and the decisions (= one per 0.533 s of sim,
  i.e. ~4/episode instead of ~64, -4.3 s of the 28.6 s wall).
