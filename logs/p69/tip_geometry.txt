## 3. The contact invariant: what the shortening does to the grip

`lip0 = TIP_FACE - STANDOFF = 10.5 mm` is the shipped lip. The gripping face spans `[-lip, -lip + long]` about the fruit's equator, and the sphere is within the commanded interference only over `|z| <= h(r)`, so **the grip touches iff `lip >= -h(r)`** and the patch is **intact** iff `lip >= +h(r)` (P39's `neutral_bound`).

* contact patch: `h(r)` = 1.67 mm on the 2.8 cm pool minimum .. **2.64 mm on the 7.0 cm maximum** (so the largest fruit binds both bounds);
* **no contact at all** beyond `|delta| = lip0 + h_max = 13.14 mm`;
* **patch clipped** beyond `|delta| = lip0 - h_max = 7.86 mm` (P39's registered bound, which uses the 76.8 mm belt stop instead of 80.5 mm and therefore reads 4.16 mm - the conservative form).

## 4. What each magnitude buys, and where

| delta mm | belt at the **descent** (0.8 deg) | belt at the **carry** (24.6 deg, vertical part) | along-belt at the carry | **rail at the crossing** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| -0.024 | **+24.0 mm** | +21.8 mm | 10.0 mm upstream | **+0.0 mm** |
| -0.035 | **+35.0 mm** | +31.8 mm | 14.6 mm upstream | **+0.0 mm** |
| -0.045 | **+45.0 mm** | +40.9 mm | 18.7 mm upstream | **+0.0 mm** |

* **The rail number is zero by construction, not by arithmetic.** The `clr=` / `clr_win=` fields read `_payload_clearance` = the **payload's** lowest point (`fruit_z - support_radius`) against the rail top; the plate tip is `STANDOFF + r - TIP_FACE` = 24.5 .. 3.5 mm **above** the payload's bottom on the pool, so the payload sets the crossing and the tip cannot. A shorter tip raises the tip further out of the way; it moves nothing.
* **The descent number is real but unconsumed.** At the shipped aim the tip is already `r - lip0` = 3.5 .. 24.5 mm above the belt, and the descent's residual is 8.1-10.3 mm *above* the aim (a jaw error, not a tip contact). Nothing on the shipped line spends belt clearance at the tip, so the purchase is unspent.
* **The carry number is the cost side of the same coin**: the vertical part it buys is the lip it removes (1:1 with `lip` by construction), and the lip is the only material the payload has to walk out of (`logs/w8/B_RESULTS.md`, `logs/p61/P2_RESULTS.md`).

## 5. The cost side

1. **The contact patch, which is 3.3-5.3 mm tall, not 85 mm.** The plate's length is not the patch (P39 §6): the face is at `r - 0.2 mm`, so the sphere can only be within it over `|z| <= 1.67..2.64 mm`. Shortening by 24-45 mm does not shrink a contact patch - it deletes it (§3).
2. **No mass or inertia term moves.** The box is authored with `UsdPhysics.CollisionAPI` only (`_author_flat_face`, `scene.py:1332`); the finger link's `physics:mass` is authored in the asset (`assets/openarm_flat/openarm_flat_deinst.usda`), so a shorter collider changes no dynamic property. Verified at the code and the asset, not measured.
3. **The authored collider and the visual mesh diverge by `|delta|`** - the P39 A3 caveat, and here it is 24-45 mm on a 84.9 mm plate. The rendered hand is unchanged (the `visuals/` subtree is never touched), so on the **direct/policy** path the image the policy conditions on shows a hand 24-45 mm deeper than the one that collides - and P36 measured that the image channel is the one that carries the park's win.
4. **The matched pair is a null, so there is no free form of it.** `lip = (tip + delta) - (standoff - drop)`: shortening by `d` and lowering the aim by `d` leaves both `lip` and the belt clearance exactly unchanged (P39 check 2, re-asserted below). The pad asset therefore has exactly one degree of freedom and it trades lip for belt clearance 1:1.
