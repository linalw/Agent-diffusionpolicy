    rl_env reset (both presentations before this fix): active arm teleported to the
    READY pose, fruit selected by the direct selector and the belt started. The
    policy's first observation therefore has the arm at a configuration no demo
    episode ever visits (closest approach 0.220 rad), while every demo starts at

    -> the observation FIELDS match; the initial STATE does not. The env change
       is to start `direct` where the demonstrations do (grasp pose, both arms
       parked at ready like `go_ready()`), and to leave `handoff` as the harness
       reproduction.

[4] WHAT THE RECORDED ACTION IS (why a BC policy over it cannot move)

    For each demo: median over frames of ||action[:7] - measured q|| and
    ||action[:7] - next measured q||; a hold/brake command is ~0 against the
    same-tick measured joints.

    ||action(t) - q(t)||      median 0.0114  p95 0.0127 rad
    ||action(t) - q(t+1)||    median 0.0115  p95 0.0426 rad

    The scripted recorder stores the MEASURED joints during the wait/close/carry
    (tasks.py `_record(self._action9(arm.joint_positions(), arm))`) and the
    calibrated grasp_config during the wait. Commanding a measured-joint action
    back is a brake, so the arm motion in a demo is not encoded in its action
    column at all. That is a property of (a), not a field mismatch; the expert
    labels DAgger aggregates are the missing command signal.
