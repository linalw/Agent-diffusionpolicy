the profile can ever ask for, so no joint velocity limit is reachable.

What the effort cap *does* do: the drive saturates at `sat.err` = 1.75 mrad
(j1/2), 1.18 mrad (j3/4) or 0.31 mrad (j5/6/7) of error, and the measured
8.1-10.4 mm tool lag at a ~0.3 m lever is ~30 mrad - 17-95x past saturation. So on
every real leg the arm drive is a *torque source*, not a stiffness, which is
why `sat.err` is not a speed number and why the gains are not a rate lever.

G. THE FOLLOWING ERROR, AND THE TWO CAP CLASSES (clock vs convergence)
================================================================================================
A leg whose duration is a profile length is *clocked*: the ticks are fixed by
`jerk_limited`, so the drive's bandwidth can only change how *well* the leg
tracks, never how long it takes. `end=` is that tracking error:

`v_end*tau` is the first-order bound on the following error at the leg's end
with the asset's tau = 0.200 s. The historical corpus (logs/161, the indexed
descent commanded into the belt) reads 42-88 mm with v_end 0.002-0.013 m/s - that
gap is a *boundary stop*, not a lag, and logs/165 measured it unchanged under
FRUIT_TUNE_GAINS=1 (36-98 mm), which is why the gains are not a speed lever.

The convergence-bound blocks (duration set by tau and the move's tolerance):
  hover 0.74-0.86 s = move_to(max_steps=300, tol 0.008 m) from ~6 cm above the
  pick point + the 20-tick settle; return 0.50-0.67 s = the same loop to `ready`.
  Both are *not* profile-clocked, so these are the only blocks a drive gain could
  shorten - and `FRUIT_HOVER_STEP` 0.04 rad/tick is not what bounds them: 0.04 rad
  at a 0.3 m lever is 12 mm/tick, i.e. the 6 cm hover is ~5 ticks of travel
  against the 82-103 measured. The binder is the 8 mm tolerance under tau.

The finger drive's own following error at the close (P40, logs/p40/EFFORT_READ.md
`err` = drive_target - position): 3.95-7.57 mm (median 5.59) at k = 1e6 N/m, i.e.
the authored k*err is 4-8 kN and the 10 N/joint cap clips every row; at the P41
profile (k = 1e5) the same err is 40-76x the 0.1 mm saturation error. The finger
cap is therefore *not* the limiter - the delivered pinch is (9.8 N of 20).
