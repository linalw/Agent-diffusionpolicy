P112 algebra - does a three-jaw ring centre where the four-jaw cannot?
==========================================================================================================
  pool (logs/p110/fixture.txt): 16 fruits, d 29.6-69.6 mm, m 10-123 g
  pad-payload friction mu_pair = (2.0 + mu_fruit)/2 = 1.25-1.46
  ring: 3 pads at 3x120 deg, ONE scalar aperture, cap 4.90 N/pad, plate 20 x 60 mm, lip 10.5 mm, aperture 120 -> 26 mm
  squeeze s = 0.01 d per face (FRUIT_GRIPPER_SQUEEZE 0.98); close clock 24+60 ticks, min-jerk

1. THE CONTACT SET: three pads hold to 2s, four lose the far pad at s
==========================================================================================================
  offset `delta` toward pad 0, penetration p_k = s + delta cos(theta_k):
    4 pads (0/90/180/270): p = s+d, s, s-d, s     -> the 180 pad leaves at d = s
    3 pads (0/120/240)   : p = s+d, s-d/2, s-d/2 -> all three hold to d = 2s
  Every loaded 3-jaw pad has an oblique normal, i.e. a tangential (friction)
  component AND a restoring component; two opposed 4-jaw pads have neither.
    n=4 at delta=1.04 mm (1.5 s): pads loaded 3/4
    n=3 at delta=1.04 mm (1.5 s): pads loaded 3/3

2. THE STATIC FIXED POINT (the kill's number)
==========================================================================================================
  drive D = -sum N_k u_k; resistance along m = D/|D| is
    mu * sum_k N_k * sqrt(1-(m.u_k)^2) + mu_b*m*g
  The sqrt term is the **tangential projection**, and it is the term P111's
  closed forms dropped.  `delta_inf/s < 1` = the payload reaches the ring's
  own squeeze circle (the `delta < s` condition of the 4-contact state).

     not the 3-4x P111's paragraph implied.

3. THE CAP BRANCH: k is a WINDOW, not a slope
==========================================================================================================
  `N_k = min(k p_k, cap)` saturates the *near* pad (p = s + delta) first:
  drive falls, friction does not, the fixed point moves OUTWARD.  Two bounds:
    k >= 0.5 N / (s - delta_inf/2)   the two narrow pads must COUNT (0.5 N)
    k <= cap / (s + delta_inf)        the wide pad must not saturate early

   29.6 1.25   0.30      2908      9005     3.10x
   29.6 1.46   0.30      3113      8645     2.78x
   49.6 1.25   0.50      1736      5374     3.10x

  THE CHOSEN POINT: k_close = **3383 N/m** (the window's geometric mean), c = 16.5 Ns/m (= 2 sqrt(k m))
  hold k = 1e5 N/m (P111's hard stop) -> 14.70 N over three pads; P104's licensed axial capacity `n mu N` = 19.1 N against the

4. THE DYNAMIC CLOSE: capture is the binding term, and it is the bat
==========================================================================================================
  the aperture ramps at the task's clock while the payload is free on the
  table; the pads are force-capped springs, the contacts and the table are
  Coulomb, the payload is a point mass (no rotation, no deformation).
  `captured` = it ends inside the squeeze circle with all three pads >= 0.5 N.

     2.0 mm       0.46    2.46     2    NO   |     0.12    2.12     4    yes
     5.0 mm       1.98    6.98     2    NO   |     0.07    5.07     4    yes

  the freeze is the protocol's own binding term: the depth is frozen at the
  FIRST touch (0.5 N for 3 ticks), which with an arrival offset happens
  BEFORE the ring is inside the fruit's widest extent - so the pads hold an
  aperture that is `2*|delta0|` too wide and the carry starts at ~0 N

  -> with the shipped freeze, neither ring captures an arrival beyond a few
     tenths of a millimetre: the freeze converts the arrival offset into a
     **held aperture error** of `2*delta0`, which is the cage family's
     ejection/zero-force class (P107/P108/P111), and it is the same defect
     for three pads and four.  The fixture therefore runs **both** protocols
     (freeze / full close): the centring question needs the full close, and
     the delivered question needs the freeze.

5. THE KILL: is delta_inf >= s at the measured mu?
==========================================================================================================
  worst delta_inf/s over the pool and mu 1.25-1.46 = **0.915**
  -> the kill **DOES NOT FIRE**.  The 3-jaw
     centres by the algebra, so the build and the fixture are owed.

7. PREDICTIONS (registered as F1-F5 in logs/p112/PREREG.md)
==========================================================================================================
  P1 CENTRING: median `off - off0` **negative** on the in-ring rows
     (-0.3..-6.4 mm predicted), against P111's +0.2..+2.0 mm for four pads.
  P2 ENCLOSURE: the clean 3-pad count (P110's jam/over-closure split) rises
     above the cage family's 3-5 % (P111: 3/76 rigid at best).
  P3 THE COUNT: placed rises above the rigid 4-pad ring's 25.0 % (19/76).
  P4 NO CRUSH: `sep@loss - dia >= -15 mm` on every leg.
  P5 LANDING + BUDGETS: reported, never re-tuned (AGENTS section 2).

  HARDWARE PRICE (registered before the build): a 3-jaw ring is a
  **different geometry** - three pads at 120 deg on one radial synergy.  The
  four-pad cage bolts to the flange through one adapter plate; a 3-jaw needs
  its own plate, three radial guides and a scroll disc (or three links from
  one linear actuator) to drive all three from one scalar.  The pads are
  20 x 60 mm blocks (width <= the 26 mm aperture, length <= the palm's
  20.5 mm stand-off) in POM or 6082; the cost is the single-scalar
  mechanism, not the pads.  A one-off machined assembly.
