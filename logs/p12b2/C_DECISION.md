# P12-C DECISION (2026-10-18): the mass base is the two-line's iteration reference; the shipped-default flip is deferred

## The decision

1. **The mass realism (`FRUIT_PHYSICS_REAL_MASS=1`) becomes the two-line's iteration
   reference**: the anchor for the next arcs' batteries is the mass base (45/60, the falls 10,
   deterministic - the double-run bit-identical). The two-line scenario batteries pass the env
   explicitly; the reference records are the mass-base runs (`logs/p12a2/10..12_mass`).
2. **No shipped-default flip now.** The mass flip would touch the single-arm line too (the
   masses are global), and the single-arm's response is UNTESTED (the owner's directive scoped
   the iteration measurements to the two-line anchor). A flip therefore needs its own
   acceptance + re-baseline - the deferred step, gated on the owner or a dedicated batch.
3. **The recorded alternatives**: the S4 containment (the falls 10 -> 1; the outcomes -13) is
   the safety option; the rest-only (38, the falls 5) is the falls-leaning option; the
   compliance is closed (-9). All stay default-off, recorded.
4. **The arc's remaining named items** (for the next arc, not this one): the mass base + a
   fresh lever family (the physics lane is exhausted at the mass; the scene/grip geometry
   closed; the containment refinement; the policy-side items #6/#10 of the mechanism menu);
   the single-arm's mass response (a dedicated batch); the two-line reference re-record on any
   default flip.

## Why (the arc's measured basis)

The P12 arc: the mass +6 (deterministic); every other lever tested on it trades worse (the
compound 35, the S3 32, the compliance-only 30, the S4 32-with-1-fall, the rest 38); the
geometry family closes (the S3 screen); the containment works but re-draws. The mass base is
the first deterministic two-line configuration above the shipped anchor since the monitor+gate
adoption.
