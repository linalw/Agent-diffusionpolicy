1. the [carry] clearance thresholds (|y|, m)
   main belt .. 0.4000  the surface switch (the removed rail's INNER face)
   0.4000 .. 0.4125  the rail's former 25 mm footprint (a void now)
   0.4125 .. 0.4250  still inboard of the slab: `clr_win` charges it, conservatively
   >= 0.4250                 the slab's own inboard edge -> `clr_edge` samples here
   (rail inset 0.015 m; rail box height 0.050 m, proud 0.035 m)

2. the surfaces that exist with FRUIT_OUTPUT_RAILS=none
   output_rail_keep = (False, False) -> `_payload_clearance`'s rail branch (`y <= 0.46 and keep[0]`) is never entered
   main belt top      1.170 m (|y| < 0.400 - inboard of the crossing)
   output belt top    1.350 m (the slab, |y| >= 0.425)
   => contact requires the payload's lowest point BELOW the top plane while
      horizontally OVER the slab, i.e. `clr_edge <= 0`. No raised surface exists,
      so no +N mm rail convention transfers to this frame.

3. the crossing height and its two slopes
   z_transfer = output_place_z + hand = 1.450 + 0.08 = 1.530 m (moves 1:1 with the belt top)
   z_lift_end = main belt top + 0.28 - 0.08 = 1.370 m (anchored to the MAIN belt: invariant in the output belt top)
   rail centreline (clr)      |y|=0.4125 f=0.825 -> z_cross=1.502 clr=+152.0 mm | d clr/d(lift)=+0.175 d clr/d(belt top)=-0.175
   the P72 edge (clr_edge)    |y|=0.4250 f=0.850 -> z_cross=1.506 clr=+156.0 mm | d clr/d(lift)=+0.150 d clr/d(belt top)=-0.150
   why: the transfer follows the belt 1:1 while the lift end is anchored to the
        main belt, so only (1-f) of a lift change reaches the crossing and only
        f of a belt change does, against an obstacle that moves 1:1 -> |0.175|
        (0.15 at the edge). Neither lever reaches a zero crossing without the
        placements (P66 P4, P70 cell B).
   the ABSOLUTE above is a straight-line envelope, not a prediction: the
        achieved transfer descends early, so P71's measured `clr` at the rail
        centreline is +14..+50 mm - 100-140 mm below the envelope. The slopes
        are the derivable content; the value is measured (`clr_edge`).
