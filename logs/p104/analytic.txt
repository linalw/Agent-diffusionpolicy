P104 analytic critical axial force - Coulomb, independent contacts, no peel
  N_c = 4.90 N per contact   (measured pinch, P41)
  a   = 1.089 m/s^2           (shipped lift/place cap)

== (1) the tangential mode: F_crit = n * mu * N_c ==

form                    n   F(0.45)  ratio   F(1.13)  ratio   F(1.45)  ratio 
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) two-pad pinch       2      4.41   1.00     11.07   1.00     14.21   1.00 
(b) 3-contact tripod    3      6.62   1.50     16.61   1.50     21.32   1.50 
(c) 4-contact           4      8.82   2.00     22.15   2.00     28.42   2.00 

  ratios are mu-free: 3 contacts = 1.50x, 4 contacts = 2.00x, by arithmetic.
  the 1.5x bar is therefore cleared by (b) exactly at its letter and by (c)
  with a 0.5x margin - in *this* model, for *any* mu.

== (2) the normal mode: a distal lip ==

  (d) two-pad pinch + distal ledge: the pull is carried by a contact
      normal anti-parallel to it, so the axial capacity is the lip's own
      strength (or the jaw's), not mu * N. Ideal ratio: unbounded.

== (3) the load the cell actually applies, beside those capacities ==

  The measured pinch is 11.0-14.2 N (fixture), i.e. 10-20x the demand, and

  the binding term. That is what the fixture's creep column is for.
