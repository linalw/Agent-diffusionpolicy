== the [bed-sel] rows name the aperture (attempt 3 of the Q12 run) ==
  5 rows  "below window along=... floor"      along -0.514..-0.097 (past the station)
  4 rows  "below catch floor 0.399"           along +0.125..+0.284 (too close)
  2 rows  "usable"                            along +0.402, +0.424

=> the catchable band is ~[0.399, ~0.55] m = ~0.15 m = 2-4 fruit, while one
   15 s pick traverses 1.8 m of belt (15 fruit). The fruit that remain on the
   belt after a pick are either past the station or inside the 0.399 m floor;
   the queue interlock is NOT the binding term (raising it to 12 changes neither
   the placed count nor the gap classes).

CONCLUSION: the continuous episode is starve-limited by the pick's own belt
consumption (12x the catchable band), not by the feeder's release cadence. The
terms that could move it are the station geometry (catch floor / band length),
the belt speed, or a staged queue - each a scenario change with its own
pre-registration and reference re-records.
