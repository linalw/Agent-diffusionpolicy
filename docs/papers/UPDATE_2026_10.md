## 2. The scouted findings (2024–2026) with the transfer sketches

The five mission topics; every item: URL in `logs/p17/C_EVIDENCE.md` §3. Sketch fields per the

### 2a. The capture/grip transient under belt motion

**A1. IMA-Catcher (Tassi et al., IJRR 2025, arXiv 2506.20801) — FULL.**
*What to port:* the two-phase catch structure as a *designed* split of our
descent→close→hold: pre-catching velocity matching (QP, real-time) + post-catching energy
dissipation (human-learned variable stiffness). Its impact model is the cleanest external
statement of our window: **F̂ ∝ relative velocity / (reflected inertia + object mass)** — VM is
the dominant term, and **stiffness cannot affect the impact itself** (few-ms duration); it
shapes only the post-contact phase.
*Bottleneck link:* the capture transient (the hold walk).
*Expected movement:* bounded — and that is the finding. Our VM is already measured-matched at
the close start (H1: the hand at belt speed on both classes; B1 handoffs *cleaner* than the
oks'), so the VM half has no measured headroom; the POC half is the M2 family (measured:
mechanism win, class trade). The one untried piece is reflected-mass minimization (reconfigure
the arm to reduce the effective mass along the contact normal) — **not transferable to this
sim stack**: our arm is position-controlled and the catch has no binding joint-torque limit
(the budgets are kinematic).
*Cost:* L for the control-stack half; S to cite the VM/POC framing in the P17-B pre-reg.
*Falsification:* a reflected-mass lever has no actuator here; the VM endpoint is already
measured (hand at belt speed, B1 matched).

**A2. Outcome-Sensitive Motion Search (Pei et al., arXiv 2609.29020, Sep 2026) — FULL.**
*What to port:* (i) the **OSW concept** — "the influence of an action on the final outcome is
highly nonuniform along a catching trajectory: small variations shortly before contact can
substantially alter interception, impact, and subsequent grasping" — an independent
formulation of our capture window; (ii) the **construction method**: local search over a
successful-motion manifold, *localized to the window*, validated by complete rollouts
(refinement improves impact quality, repair converts failed conditions; student 90.3 % >
teacher 85.2 %; xArm7 + ORCA hand).
*Bottleneck link:* the no-grasp class (the hold + probe-1 onsets). For us the window's actions
are the *scripted primitive's* — which is the good news: unlike a policy's actions, the
close/hold profile is ours to construct offline.
*Expected movement:* the walk and the no-grasp class (the only classes the trace localizes
there); the carried-class check is mandatory (the P15 lesson).
*Cost:* S–M (the profile parameters already exist: `FRUIT_CLOSE_LOAD_RATE`,
`FRUIT_CLOSE_CONTACT_LEAD`, the freeze criterion, the probe shape; the validation is the
existing battery).
*Falsification:* the walk does not move; or it moves with the carried class up and the pooled
outcomes flat/worse (the M2 signature); any descent over budget; a re-seat rise.

**A3. How Firm Should a Grasp Be? (Beveridge et al., CoRL 2026) — BODY-SNIP.**
*What to port:* (i) the **minimum-grasp-force** quantity: estimate the object's mass/friction
during grip/lift and hold the least force that prevents slip (nectarine optimum 1.12 N; a
29-fruit measured-property dataset; GelSight Minis + load cells on a UR5/WSG50); (ii) the
**drop-test** measurement form: reduce force until optical-flow-detected slip → µ and
interfacial shear τ. Our offline analogue already exists as the named friction-cone feasibility
audit over carry logs (MECHANISMS §2c); the new half is using the margin **at selection**.
*Bottleneck link:* the marginal-grip population (the capture's required force vs the drive
cap).
*Expected movement:* selection-side only — fewer marginal-grip presentations when the lane has
a choice; bounded by P1c (composition dominates; class-standardize).
*Cost:* S (offline: the sim's µ and masses are known; replay the selection instants for
margin choices).
*Falsification:* the offline replay shows the selection instants rarely offer a margin choice
(the shipped rule takes the farthest-upstream eligible fruit) → dead; or the margin choice
re-draws the class with no conversion.

**A4. FruitTouch (arXiv 2602.18991, 2026) — BODY-SNIP.**
*What to port:* the **slip-triggered, force-guided retry**: on detected slip, close an extra
2 mm and retry; force targets step (2 N + 1 N). Measured: strawberry 12.5 % (open) → 37.5 %
(slip) → **75 % (slip+force)**; cherry tomato 58.3 % → 100 %.
*Bottleneck link:* the probe-cycle completion (our W1 two-line profile already re-seats in
place after a failed probe; the single-arm line's re-seat windows do not initiate losses — F1).
*Expected movement:* small on the two-line (the W1 form ships); the untested bit is
force-guided vs position-guided increments — **capped by R6-B**: the lift command already sits
11.9 mm inside the payload span and the relief cap leaves the force unchanged (drive-saturated
pinch).
*Cost:* S.
*Falsification:* the `F_min = 0`/`closed-at-range` servo signature persists (the drives, not
the command, are the limit) — already measured on our line.

**A5. Shear-based Grasp Control (arXiv 2503.17501, 2025) — ABS; Adaptive Grasp-Force Tracking
(arXiv 2412.02335, 2025) — ABS; Bombile & Billard grabbing-with-impact (RAM 2022) — ABS.**
*What to port:* the shear/tangential loop (zero-shear setpoint; PID on ΔF_x/ΔF_y) and the
online stiffness-adaptive force tracker. Both need a **shear-capable tactile channel**; our sim
tactile exposes normal force + contact count only (the standing bound), and the in-window
reaction family is closed (W5/R11-B + the A1/A2 few-ms/pre-contact confirmations).
*Bottleneck link:* the capture transient's tangential load — the exact quantity our trace
measures as the hand-frame walk.
*Expected movement:* none without a sim tactile extension; recorded as the *named requirement*
if the sim-side tactile ever grows (the falsifier is the 0–2 tick window itself).
*Cost:* M (sim extension) + the closed reaction family.

### 2b. The conserved failure mass / trade phenomenon

**B1. Cracking Mobile Manipulation (Wang et al., arXiv 2610.19600, Oct 2026) — FULL.**
*What to port:* the **moving failure frontier** concept and its accounting protocol:
fine-grained failure attribution per stage (planning 21.0 % / navigation-base 25.9 % / local
manipulation 53.1 % of 81 failures), then **track the failure distribution shift as
interventions land** — "as planning and navigation bottlenecks are alleviated, more episodes
reach later stages, causing manipulation to emerge as the dominant remaining failure type";
targeted modes 9→0, 11→1, 10→3 under 19 % → 47 % success; attribution rules = recovered errors
(within the next replans) excluded; the earliest unresolved error attributed; triple-model
diagnosis + human review (κ=0.86).
*Bottleneck link:* the measurement layer (all of the map) — this is the closest external
formulation of our conservation finding. Honest distinction: their frontier *moves downstream
and the total falls*; ours is conserved *within* the close/carry family at a fixed stage. The
transferable part is the **reporting discipline** (publish the shift per stage), not a rate
claim.
*Expected movement:* none (protocol); it makes the P15-style trade visible before a rate is
quoted.
*Cost:* S (the class census + falls + onset tables already exist; add the from→to column).
*Falsification:* the shift table contradicts the census → the attribution is wrong; re-derive
(the P14-B pairing rules are the check).

**B2. GTP-FA (Wang et al., arXiv 2606.03385, Aug 2026) — FULL.**
*What to port:* **failure attribution as an optimization-routing signal**: failure modes
{task-irrelevant grasp, unstable grasp/slip, downstream policy}; weak labels from **K repeated
trials under a fixed grasp condition** (terminal success rate — our double-runs are the same
instrument); a discriminator + a grasp-conditioned embedding for unseen conditions; then
*route* the update pressure — grasp-side failures must not contaminate the policy update and
vice versa. Ablations are the load-bearing part: planning-only optimization *hurts* PPO/SAC
(−27.9/−54.5 pp), grasp-only hurts everyone, **naive joint optimization still hurts PPO/SAC** —
attribution is the enabling mechanism (PPO +31.3, BC +54.0, DP +25.7, π0.5 +17.8 pp; real
robot 11.2 % → 76.8 %).
*Bottleneck link:* the policy-side iteration (the R9 lesson is exactly a misrouted update: the
post-training moved the handover channel it should not have degraded) + the store curation
lane.
*Expected movement:* the acquisition/handover endpoints (ev1's channel), with the carry class
explicitly *not* targeted (scripted-owned); a possible paired conversion if the attribution is
right.
*Cost:* S–M (the attributed labels come from existing diag rows — the W7-B fire-path classes,
the P14-B phases; one curation + one fine-tune).
*Falsification:* the policy-attributable subset is empty (all failures scripted-side → the
policy lane is capped at the acquisition channel); no N=5 paired conversion; the canary
separation at ≥5 runs/side.

**B3. RoboMD (Sagar et al., ICLR 2026, arXiv 2412.02818) — ABS.**
*What to port:* active RL search for failure-inducing conditions in a VLM embedding. Our
scenario space is enumerable and cheap (seeds × supply × configs), so the search adds nothing;
recorded as the diagnosis-family context. *Cost:* n/a. *Falsification:* our seeds are the
search.

### L2 — The attribution-routed policy iteration (the GTP-FA form on the ev1 channel)
